I am a postdoctoral researcher in physics at [Leiden University](https://www.universiteitleiden.nl/en/science/physics), where I work with [Jordi Tura](https://jtura.cat). My research aims to understand, control, and optimize quantum systems, with a particular focus on quantum thermodynamics, quantum nonlocality, and quantum many-body physics.

## Research Interests

### Quantum thermodynamics

During my Ph.D., I focused on quantum thermodynamics and finite-time optimization. I developed control schemes for thermodynamic processes operating away from the quasistatic limit, showing how the power and efficiency of quantum heat engines can be improved when only a limited operation time is available.

### Quantum nonlocality

I have studied optimization problems across quantum information, quantum computing, quantum thermodynamics, and statistical physics. More recently, I have combined tools from group theory and algebraic topology to analyze and optimize multipartite Bell inequalities, with the goal of identifying stronger and more structured manifestations of quantum nonlocality.

### Quantum many-body systems

I also study relaxation and thermalization in open quantum many-body systems. By optimizing the spectral gap of the Lindbladian and using tensor-network and DMRG methods, I design and analyze efficient strategies for accelerating thermalization.

Before joining Leiden in February 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Peking University in the group of [H. T. Quan](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=PTULG-sAAAAJ), working on mesoscopic quantum and classical systems, fluctuation theorems, and control optimization. I completed my Ph.D. in quantum physics in 2021 at the Beijing Computational Science Research Center (CSRC), under the supervision of [C. P. Sun](http://suncp.gscaep.ac.cn). I received my B.S. in Physics from Peking University in 2016, together with a double degree in Mathematics.
