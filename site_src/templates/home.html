03 / ABOUT ME

A geometric perspective
on the quantum world

I am a postdoctoral researcher at Leiden University’s Lorentz Institute, working with Jordi Tura. My research connects quantum information, nonequilibrium statistical physics, and optimal control.

My path began with physics and mathematics at Peking University, followed by a Ph.D. at the Beijing Computational Science Research Center and postdoctoral research with H. T. Quan at Peking University.
